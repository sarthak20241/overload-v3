Overload · Coach chat sketch

One orb, three moments

Drona gets a living orb. It is big while you wait for an answer, and small while you read one. Press play, or type in the phone yourself.

  1. 1
    Empty chatThe orb sits large at the top and breathes slowly. Start typing and it wakes up.
  2. 2
    First messageThe orb stays large and says what Drona is doing: thinking, looking up your sets, checking your trends.
  3. 3
    Words arriveThe moment the reply starts, the orb shrinks and flies out of the way. The words get the room. It keeps showing status on every later turn, from its small spot.

Where the small orb lives

While you wait, the orb rises big above the text box with the status under it, and a soft light comes up from below. When words arrive, it drops into the text box and pulses with each word.
