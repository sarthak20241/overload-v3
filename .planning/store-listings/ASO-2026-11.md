## The big problem: both stores say "free"

- App Store text: "Tracking is free forever: unlimited workouts ... plus three
  coach messages and three AI food logs every day."
- Play text: "FREE TO START. Tracking workouts and logging food from the
  catalog is free, with 3 coach chats and 3 AI food logs per rolling 24 hours."

Since the hard paywall (2026-11-09), every NEW account must start a trial or
pay before it sees the app (`lib/hardPaywall.ts`). So a new person reads
"free forever", installs, and meets a locked paywall. That earns 1-star
reviews, and Apple can reject a build for metadata that does not match the app
(guideline 2.3.1). Fix it first, on both stores.

### Title: DECIDED 2026-11-14, one name on both stores

```text
Overload: AI Coach & Tracker
```

"Overload: Gym Log & AI Coach" was live on Play for about 2 hours on
2026-11-14, then reverted (all 4 locales, read back). Why the old name wins:

- Google Trends, US, 12 months, relative: "ai workout" about 50, "workout
  tracker" 12, "gym tracker" about 7, "gym log" about 5, "ai fitness coach"
  about 3. "Gym log" is the weakest word of the set.
- Play search already ranked the app with the old title: #1 for "overload ai
  coach", #2 for "overload", #4 for "ai coach tracker".
- App Store search "overload": the app is #26, behind a crowd of low-rating
  apps named "Overload: Workout Tracker", "Overload: Lift Tracker",
  "Overload: Workout Logger", "Overload: Gym Log". "AI Coach" is what sets
  the name apart; "Gym Log" would copy an app that already exists.
- The App Store subtitle and the Play short description already carry gym,
  workout, log, calorie and counter.
- One name everywhere (stores, website JSON-LD, ChatGPT and MCP listings).

No keyword-volume tool was available (Ahrefs plan too low), so Trends is a
proxy for store search, not a measure of it.
